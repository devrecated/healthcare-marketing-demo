# 05 — Confirm & realtime

## Goal

Human-in-the-loop confirm on the phone; laptop supplies drop via Realtime.

## Surfaces (split or picture-in-picture)

- Phone: scan MFE review → **Confirm & deduct all**
- Laptop: https://healthcare-demo.devrecated.com/inventory

## Shot list

1. Phone: **Done capturing — review** → matches list.
2. Phone: tap **Confirm & deduct all** (or per-form Deduct).
3. Cut or PiP to laptop Supplies — quantity decrease and/or “Just deducted from scan” banner.
4. Hold 2–3 seconds so the live update is obvious.

## On-screen copy to capture

- Confirm / deducted success on phone
- Updated on-hand quantity on laptop

## Do not show

- Failed CORS / wrong `VITE_API_BASE` toasts (fix tunnels before recording).
- Service-role keys or Network tab payloads.
