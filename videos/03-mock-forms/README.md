# 03 — Mock forms

## Goal

Show the printable Acme fixture path a presenter uses before the phone scan.

## Surface

- Host: https://healthcare-demo.devrecated.com/inventory/intake

## Shot list

1. Open **Mock forms**.
2. Highlight one catalog form (e.g. Richmond — knee) with Acme Healthcare header.
3. Either **Print** / download, or hold the on-screen form preview clearly.
4. Optional: click **Generate random** once to show synthetic variety.

## On-screen copy to capture

- “Acme Healthcare” on the form header
- Patient ref like `ACME-RVA-4471` (synthetic)

## Do not show

- Any real patient sticker or unredacted clinical document.
- Upload of PHI “just for the video.”
