# 04 — Phone scan

## Goal

Capture the phone microfrontend: sign in, photograph (or upload) a form, wait for extract.

## Surface

- Scan MFE: https://healthcare-docscan-mfe-demo.devrecated.com  
- Record on a phone or a narrow mobile emulator; prefer a real device for camera credibility.

## Shot list

1. Login screen — “Acme Healthcare · Scan”, password `acme`, **Open camera**.
2. Camera preview (or show **Add photos** if recording from desktop).
3. Capture / select the printed or on-screen Acme fixture.
4. Hold the **Queue** while status moves through extracting → ready/error.

## On-screen copy to capture

- “Acme Healthcare · Scan”
- “Device form camera”
- Queue row for the captured form

## Do not show

- Home-screen icons from other clients.
- Notifications with patient names from other apps.
